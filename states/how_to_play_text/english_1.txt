HOW TO PLAY

Your Mission: Don’t let the board explode, implode, overload, 
or generally ruin your day.

1. You Are the Diamond Block!
See that fancy block with the diamond? 
That’s you. You can’t walk anywhere, 
but you can magically respawn like you’re late for work. 
Press R1 before the timer yells at you.

2. The Selector
This is your little cursor buddy. It only moves up, down, left, right.
No diagonals. It’s allergic. 
Put it over a block to threaten it with destruction.

3. Destroying Blocks
Every block shows a specific face button.
Hit the right one → block goes poof.
Hit the wrong one → a bad block appears and the board speeds up,
like it had too much coffee.

HOW TO PLAY

FATE BLOCKS
Purple ? blocks appear during the game.
Destroy one before it disappears to trigger a random Fate.

Some Fates help you.
Some make survival much harder.
Learn them and adapt.


L1 — RISK AND REWARD
Press L1 / Left Shoulder to immediately spawn 6 bad blocks.
Doing so starts a bonus timer.
Press L1 again before the timer expires and your multiplier doubles:
x2  x4  x8  x16  x32  x64...
Each successful L1 press also extends the bonus timer.
While the bonus is active, destroyed blocks earn additional points.
The higher you push the multiplier,
the more dangerous the board becomes.

Play safe and survive...

or push your luck for a massive score.

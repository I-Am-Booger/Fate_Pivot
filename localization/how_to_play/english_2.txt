HOW TO PLAY

SCORING
Destroying blocks earns points.
As your level increases, normal blocks become worth more.


COMBOS
Destroy 3 blocks of the same color in a row to trigger a combo.

RED
Clears bad blocks across your row.

BLUE
Clears bad blocks in your column.

GREEN
Restores time, slows the bad-block timer,
and helps extend an active L1 bonus.

YELLOW
Destroys up to 6 random bad blocks.

LOSING
The run ends if your score reaches -50
or the board becomes completely full.
When the board fills, everything goes with it.

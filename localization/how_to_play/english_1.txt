HOW TO PLAY

YOUR GOAL
Survive as long as you can, destroy blocks, build your score,
and keep the board from filling completely.


THE WHITE DIAMOND
The white diamond is you.
Move the selector with the D-Pad or WASD.
Destroy an adjacent colored block by pressing the face button
shown on that block.


RESPAWNING
Your player has a timer.
When the timer reaches zero, your player shatters, your score
takes a penalty, and you respawn somewhere else on the board.
You can also respawn early with R1 / Right Shoulder.


WRONG BUTTONS
Pressing the wrong face button is dangerous.
A new bad block spawns, your score penalty increases,
and a small amount of player time is lost.
Repeated mistakes become increasingly expensive.
